I am Fredrik Rundgren, a data and analytics leader with a background in data engineering, operations, and architecture. I currently lead Redpill Linpro Data Analytics Stockholm.

I have spent much of my career at the point where technical design meets the reality of operating a service. A data platform can look convincing in a diagram and still fail the people who depend on it. Clear ownership, trustworthy inputs, observable behavior, and the ability to improve a system without losing control matter as much as the initial build.

Before Redpill Linpro, I worked at SQL Service Nordic and Cegal. At Cegal I led work on Data Analytics as a Service, with an emphasis on reliable delivery and shared ways of working. That experience sharpened a question that continues to shape my work: how do we turn a sequence of successful projects into a durable organisational capability?

My current interests include data products and contracts, governance that can be checked by machines, open standards, digital sovereignty, and the practical use of AI in organisations. I am developing an independent research agenda around these questions. I want to distinguish clearly between a useful hypothesis, an implemented tool, and an evaluated result.

I build in public when I can. Open source is a way to expose assumptions, invite criticism, and give others a starting point they can inspect and adapt. The [projects](/open-source/) and [research notes](/research/) on this site show where that work stands, including its limits.

I also care about explaining technical ideas across disciplines. Good architecture requires people with different responsibilities to understand the same decision well enough to challenge it. [Writing](/writing/) and [speaking](/speaking/) are part of that work.
