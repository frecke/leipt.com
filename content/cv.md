## Profile

I am CEO and Partner of Redpill Linpro Data Analytics Stockholm AB. My work spans data engineering, platform architecture, information modelling, service operations, and consulting leadership. I help organisations turn data ambitions into dependable capabilities: systems that people can understand, operate, and improve.

My approach combines technical depth with clear ownership and practical delivery. I have moved from hands-on development and database work into leading teams and services, while staying close to the architecture and engineering decisions that shape long-term outcomes.
