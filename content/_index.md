## Useful systems are built to last

I lead Redpill Linpro Data Analytics Stockholm. My work sits between data engineering, analytics, architecture, and the people who have to make these systems useful every day.

I am interested in what happens after a promising prototype: who owns the data, how quality is established, what can be changed safely, and whether the capability still works when the original team has moved on.

This site is a public record of the work I can share, the [open projects](/open-source/) I am building, and the [questions](/research/) I am still working through. It is also a place to make the reasoning visible: not just conclusions, but the choices and trade-offs behind them.
