Speaking

I have led talks and workshops for customers in consulting roles, including at Atollo. I am now developing that experience into public speaking. This page is a starting point for the topics I would like to take to a wider audience; it is not a claim that I already have a public conference track record.

Proposed talks

From data project to data capability

What changes when analytics becomes a service people rely on every day? A practical talk about ownership, quality, operations, and the team habits that keep a platform useful.

Governance as evidence, not paperwork

How can contracts, metadata, provenance, and policy checks help people make better decisions? This talk uses open DataGovOps work to separate what can be automated from what still needs accountable human judgment.

Open systems and meaningful control

Open source alone does not guarantee autonomy. This talk explores the architectural and organisational choices that determine whether a team can understand, move, and improve its systems.

AI beyond the demonstration

How do teams move from an impressive AI prototype to something they can govern, operate, and improve? A proposed talk about provenance, access, evaluation, human responsibility, and the work that remains after a demo succeeds.

These are talk proposals, not a list of past public appearances. I will add recordings, slides, and event details only when they exist and are cleared for sharing.

Short speaker bio

Fredrik Rundgren leads Redpill Linpro Data Analytics Stockholm. His work connects data engineering, analytics architecture, governance, and the operational side of technology leadership. He builds public reference work around machine-readable data governance and is interested in how organisations turn technical projects into lasting capability.
