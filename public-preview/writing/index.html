Writing

I write to test an idea in public. The subjects are usually the space between a technical design and the work required to make it last: data quality, governance, operations, open technology, AI, automation, and the decisions teams make together. I also want room here to write honestly about subjects I am learning, including security, without presenting curiosity as expertise.

The first essay is in draft. I would rather publish a clear argument with its limits than a stream of finished-sounding claims. When an article appears here, I will distinguish observation from opinion and link to the work behind it where possible.
