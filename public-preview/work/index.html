Work

My professional work connects data engineering, analytics, architecture, and service ownership. I lead Redpill Linpro Data Analytics Stockholm. Previously, at Cegal, I was Head of Data Analytics as a Service and Team Lead for its delivery team.

The work I focus on

Make data usable and dependable

Data pipelines, models, and analytics services need clear purpose and a way to tell whether they are healthy. I look for explicit ownership, testable expectations, traceable changes, and operations that people can sustain.

Connect architecture to delivery

An architecture is useful when it helps teams make better decisions. I work to connect information models and governance requirements to the repositories, contracts, pipelines, controls, and feedback loops that teams actually use.

Build capability with people

Technology becomes an organisational capability when the team can operate it, explain it, and improve it. I value shared responsibility, visible decisions, and room for learning alongside reliable delivery.

What you will find here

This page describes my practice rather than naming customers or claiming outcomes I cannot document publicly. I will add case studies when the scope, my contribution, and permission to publish can all be verified. For now, the open-source work provides inspectable examples of the ideas behind my approach.
